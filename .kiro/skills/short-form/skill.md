## Idea Sourcing — Three Modes

**Mode A — Direct idea**: User gives you a concept or topic.

**Mode B — Changelog scan**: Read `/content/logs/changelog.md` and `/content/logs/episode-current.md`. Generate 5–8 short video ideas. Present and ask user to pick one.

**Mode C — Specific entry**: User points at a specific log entry as source material.

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Angle

One sentence: what is this short about and what's the hook in the first 2–3 seconds?

Show it to the user. Get confirmation before moving on.

### Step 2 — Outline

- **Hook** (first 2–3 seconds) — the thing that makes someone stop scrolling
- **Middle** (the content) — 2–4 bullet points
- **Payoff / End** — how it lands or cuts

Show the outline. Wait for approval.

### Step 3 — Script

Two-column format:

| What you say | What's on screen |
|---|---|
| Spoken words or on-screen text | Visual: gameplay, screen recording, text overlay, etc. |

Rules:
- Under 60 seconds unless content genuinely earns more
- Hook in first 2–3 rows
- Mark `[HOOK]` at start and `[END]` at close

Save to `/content/scripts/[slug]-short-script.md`.

### Step 4 — Done

When approved, it's ready to film.

## Notes

- No thumbnail/title step for short form.
- If user says "just write it" — still do a fast one-line angle confirmation first.
