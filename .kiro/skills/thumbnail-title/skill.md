## Inputs

Works from any of:
- An approved outline (preferred)
- A script summary
- A video concept in a sentence or two

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Understand the video

Summarize the video in one sentence. Confirm with user if vague.

### Step 2 — Generate options

Produce **3 title options** and **3 thumbnail concepts**.

**Title format:** Each title on its own line with one sentence explaining the angle.

**Thumbnail format:** Each concept in 2–3 sentences: what's in frame, text overlay, emotion/reaction, visual style.

### Step 3 — Present and discuss

Show all options. Ask which direction resonates or if they want variations.

## Title Principles

- Specific beats vague
- Answer "why should I watch this?"
- Numbers and timeframes add credibility
- Dev journey framing works: struggle, breakthrough, decision

## Thumbnail Principles

- One clear focal point
- Text overlay: 3–5 words max, readable on mobile
- Before/after splits work for progress moments
